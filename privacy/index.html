Your data stays with you

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Overview

Go to Sheep does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your session and lifetime sheep counts, animation progress, selected mode, pause setting, and sound setting are stored only on your device. The app uses this information to restore the experience where you left it.

This information is not transmitted to the developer or to third parties.

Permissions and third-party software

Go to Sheep does not request access to your microphone, photos, contacts, location, or other protected personal information. It uses software components for animation, audio playback, and local storage, but does not configure those components to collect or transmit your information.

Deleting your information

Deleting Go to Sheep or clearing its local storage removes its saved counts and settings. Because the app has no account or server, the developer does not hold a separate copy to delete or recover.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the Go to Sheep support page. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public support request.
