Support

Go to Sheep is designed to work entirely on your device, without an account or internet connection.

Quick answers

Where are my counts stored?
Only on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its storage removes them.
Why is there no sound?
Sound begins muted. Enable it with the music-note button. Go to Sheep also respects your device’s silent setting.
How do I reveal the controls?
Tap anywhere on the meadow. Controls fade away after a few quiet moments.
How do I reset the count?
Open Settings and choose “Begin a fresh count.” Your lifetime total is preserved.
